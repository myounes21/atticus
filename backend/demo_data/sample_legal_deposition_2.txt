IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE 11TH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT IN AND FOR MIAMI-DADE COUNTY, FLORIDA

DEPOSITION OF: JENNIFER LEE WILSON
WITNESS: JENNIFER LEE WILSON
DATE: FEBRUARY 22, 2023
REPORTER: KAREN J. SMITH, RPR

EXAMINATION BY MR. JASON P. BROWN, ESQ.

Q: Good morning, Ms. Wilson. Can you please state your full name and address for the record?

A: Good morning. My name is Jennifer Lee Wilson, and my address is 1234 SW 82nd Avenue, Miami, Florida 33144.

Q: Ms. Wilson, can you please describe your educational background and any relevant work experience you may have?

A: I have a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration from the University of Miami, which I received in 2005. After graduating, I worked as a financial analyst for a large corporation in Miami for about five years. In 2010, I decided to pursue a career in real estate and obtained my real estate license. I have been working as a real estate agent for the past 12 years.

Q: Ms. Wilson, are you familiar with the defendant, ABC Corporation?

A: Yes, I am. ABC Corporation is a large real estate development company that has been involved in several projects in the Miami area. I have had dealings with them on several occasions, both personally and professionally.

Q: Can you describe the nature of your relationship with ABC Corporation?

A: Well, as a real estate agent, I have worked with ABC Corporation on several transactions, including the sale of properties they have developed. I have also had personal dealings with them, as I have purchased a condominium unit from them in the past.

Q: Ms. Wilson, can you tell us about the condominium unit you purchased from ABC Corporation?

A: Yes. In 2015, I purchased a condominium unit at the Oceanview Towers, a development project undertaken by ABC Corporation. The purchase price was $425,000, and I financed the purchase through a mortgage loan from a local bank.

Q: Did you have any issues with the condominium unit after you purchased it?

A: Yes, I did. Shortly after I moved in, I began to notice some problems with the unit, including water leaks and cracks in the walls. I reported these issues to ABC Corporation, and they sent someone to inspect the unit. However, the problems were never fully resolved, and I continued to experience issues with the unit.

Q: Ms. Wilson, did you ever file a complaint with ABC Corporation regarding the issues you were experiencing with your condominium unit?

A: Yes, I did. In 2018, I filed a formal complaint with ABC Corporation, detailing the issues I was experiencing with my unit. I also requested that they take steps to repair the unit and compensate me for the damages I had suffered.

Q: What was the response from ABC Corporation to your complaint?

A: Initially, they acknowledged receipt of my complaint and promised to investigate the matter. However, after several months, I had not heard back from them, and I was forced to hire an attorney to pursue my claims.

Q: Ms. Wilson, can you tell us about the events that led up to the filing of this lawsuit?

A: Yes. As I mentioned earlier, I had been experiencing problems with my condominium unit, and I had filed a complaint with ABC Corporation. However, they failed to take any meaningful steps to address my concerns. In 2020, I discovered that ABC Corporation had been aware of the defects in the condominium units at the Oceanview Towers, including the unit I purchased, but had failed to disclose this information to me or other purchasers.

Q: How did you discover this information?

A: I discovered it through a public records request. I had requested documents from the City of Miami related to the Oceanview Towers development project, and in reviewing those documents, I found evidence that ABC Corporation had been aware of the defects in the units but had failed to disclose this information to purchasers.

Q: What did you do with this information once you discovered it?

A: I immediately contacted my attorney, and we began to investigate further. We discovered that several other purchasers had also experienced similar problems with their units and had also filed complaints with ABC Corporation. We realized that there was a pattern of behavior by ABC Corporation to conceal the defects in the units and to fail to disclose this information to purchasers.

Q: Ms. Wilson, did you ever have any direct dealings with any of the defendants in this case?

A: Yes, I did. I had dealings with the CEO of ABC Corporation, Mr. John Smith. He was the one who initially responded to my complaint and promised to investigate the matter. However, as I mentioned earlier, he failed to follow through on his promises, and I was forced to hire an attorney to pursue my claims.

Q: Can you describe your interactions with Mr. Smith?

A: My interactions with Mr. Smith were limited to a few phone calls and emails. Initially, he was very responsive and promised to look into my concerns. However, as time went on, he became less responsive, and I was forced to escalate my complaints to other officials within the company.

Q: Did you ever meet with Mr. Smith in person?

A: No, I did not. All of my interactions with him were over the phone or via email.

Q: Ms. Wilson, can you tell us about the damages you have suffered as a result of the defendants' actions?

A: Yes. As a result of the defects in my condominium unit, I have suffered significant financial losses. I have had to pay for repairs to the unit out of my own pocket, and I have also suffered a loss in the value of my unit due to the defects. I estimate that my total damages are in excess of $200,000.

OBJECTION BY MR. MARK D. DAVIS, ESQ., COUNSEL FOR THE DEFENDANT:

Q: Your Honor, I object to this line of questioning. The witness is not qualified to estimate the value of her damages, and this is not relevant to the issues in this case.

Q: Ms. Wilson, can you tell us about any other damages you have suffered as a result of the defendants' actions?

A: Yes. In addition to the financial losses I have suffered, I have also suffered emotional distress and anxiety as a result of the defendants' actions. The experience of dealing with the defects in my unit and the failure of ABC Corporation to address my concerns has been very stressful and has caused me a great deal of anxiety.

OBJECTION BY MR. MARK D. DAVIS, ESQ., COUNSEL FOR THE DEFENDANT:

Q: Your Honor, I object to this line of questioning. The witness is not qualified to testify about her emotional state, and this is not relevant to the issues in this case.

Q: Ms. Wilson, can you tell us about any other witnesses you are aware of who may have information relevant to this case?

A: Yes. I am aware of several other purchasers who have experienced similar problems with their units at the Oceanview Towers. I believe they may have information relevant to this case, and I am willing to provide their contact information to the court.

CROSS-EXAMINATION BY MR. MARK D. DAVIS, ESQ., COUNSEL FOR THE DEFENDANT:

Q: Ms. Wilson, isn't it true that you purchased your condominium unit at the Oceanview Towers without doing a thorough inspection of the property?

A: That's not true. I did inspect the unit before I purchased it, and I did not notice any defects at the time.

Q: But didn't you admit in your deposition that you did not hire a professional inspector to inspect the unit before you purchased it?

A: That's correct. I did not hire a professional inspector to inspect the unit. However, I did inspect the unit myself, and I did not notice any defects at the time.

Q: And didn't you also admit that you did not review the seller's disclosure statement before you purchased the unit?

A: That's correct. I did not review the seller's disclosure statement before I purchased the unit. However, I was told by the seller's agent that the unit was in good condition and that there were no known defects.

Q: Ms. Wilson, isn't it true that you have a history of filing complaints against companies and individuals?

A: I don't know what you're talking about.

Q: Haven't you filed complaints against at least three other companies in the past five years?

A: I may have filed a complaint or two, but that's not relevant to this case.

OBJECTION BY MR. JASON P. BROWN, ESQ., COUNSEL FOR THE PLAINTIFF:

Q: Your Honor, I object to this line of questioning. The witness's history of filing complaints is not relevant to the issues in this case, and this is an attempt to harass and intimidate the witness.

Q: Ms. Wilson, can you tell us about your relationship with your attorney, Mr. Jason P. Brown?

A: Mr. Brown is my attorney, and I have a professional relationship with him. He is representing me in this case, and I have confidence in his abilities.

Q: And didn't you meet with Mr. Brown on several occasions before you filed this lawsuit?

A: Yes, I did. I met with Mr. Brown to discuss my options and to determine the best course of action to take.

Q: And didn't Mr. Brown advise you to file this lawsuit against ABC Corporation?

A: Mr. Brown did advise me to file a lawsuit, but the decision to file the lawsuit was mine alone.

OBJECTION BY MR. MARK D. DAVIS, ESQ., COUNSEL FOR THE DEFENDANT:

Q: Your Honor, I object to this line of questioning. The witness's conversations with her attorney are privileged, and this is an attempt to invade the attorney-client privilege.

Q: Ms. Wilson, can you tell us about any other matters that you believe are relevant to this case?

A: I don't think so. I have answered all of the questions to the best of my ability, and I am not aware of any other matters that are relevant to this case.

OBJECTION BY MR. JASON P. BROWN, ESQ., COUNSEL FOR THE PLAINTIFF:

Q: Your Honor, I object to the defendant's attempt to harass and intimidate the witness. The witness has answered all of the questions to the best of her ability, and this deposition should be concluded.

Q: Ms. Wilson, is there anything else you would like to add to your testimony?

A: No, I don't think so. I have answered all of the questions to the best of my ability, and I am confident that my testimony is accurate and complete.
