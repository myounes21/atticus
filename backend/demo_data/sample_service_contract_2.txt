SERVICE CONTRACT

This Service Contract ("Contract") is made and entered into on this 15th day of March, 2023 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

MEDICAL TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS, INC., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 123 Main Street, Suite 400, New York, NY 10001 ("MTS");

and

HEALTHCARE PROVIDER NETWORK, LLC, a limited liability company with its principal place of business at 456 Elm Street, Suite 200, Chicago, IL 60611 ("HPN");

collectively referred to as the "Parties" and each individually referred to as a "Party".

WHEREAS, MTS is a provider of healthcare technology solutions, including software and consulting services; and

WHEREAS, HPN is a network of healthcare providers seeking to implement and utilize MTS's technology solutions to improve the delivery of healthcare services to its patients;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this Contract, the following definitions shall apply:

1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.

1.2 "Business Day" means any day, excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and federal holidays, on which banks are open for business in New York, New York.

1.3 "Confidential Information" means all information, whether written or oral, that is disclosed by one Party to the other Party, including but not limited to trade secrets, business strategies, technical data, product information, and software code.

1.4 "Contract Term" means the period of time commencing on the Effective Date and continuing for a period of three (3) years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Article 10.

1.5 "Data" means all data, including but not limited to patient data, medical records, and billing information, that is collected, stored, or transmitted by MTS or HPN in connection with the provision of the Services.

1.6 "Deliverables" means all reports, documents, and other materials that MTS is required to deliver to HPN under this Contract.

1.7 "Fees" means the fees and expenses payable by HPN to MTS under this Contract, as set forth in Article 5.

1.8 "Intellectual Property Rights" means all patents, copyrights, trade secrets, trademarks, and other intellectual property rights, whether registered or unregistered.

1.9 "MTS Software" means the software applications and programs developed and owned by MTS, including but not limited to the "MTS Healthcare Management System" and the "MTS Patient Portal".

1.10 "Services" means the consulting, implementation, and support services provided by MTS to HPN under this Contract, including but not limited to the installation, configuration, and customization of the MTS Software.

1.11 "Subcontractor" means any third-party contractor or vendor that MTS engages to provide any portion of the Services.

ARTICLE 2: SERVICES

2.1 MTS agrees to provide the Services to HPN during the Contract Term, as set forth in the Statement of Work attached as Exhibit A.

2.2 MTS shall provide the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, using qualified personnel with the necessary skills and expertise.

2.3 MTS shall be responsible for the management and supervision of all personnel engaged in the provision of the Services, including but not limited to employees, agents, and Subcontractors.

2.4 HPN shall provide MTS with timely access to all necessary information, data, and personnel, as reasonably required for the provision of the Services.

ARTICLE 3: MTS SOFTWARE

3.1 MTS grants to HPN a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the MTS Software during the Contract Term, solely for the purposes of utilizing the Services.

3.2 HPN shall not, and shall not permit any third party to, copy, modify, or create derivative works of the MTS Software, except as specifically authorized in writing by MTS.

3.3 HPN shall not, and shall not permit any third party to, reverse engineer, disassemble, or decompile the MTS Software, except as specifically authorized in writing by MTS.

3.4 MTS reserves all right, title, and interest in and to the MTS Software, including but not limited to all Intellectual Property Rights.

ARTICLE 4: DATA PRIVACY

4.1 The Parties agree to comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding the collection, storage, and transmission of Data, including but not limited to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA").

4.2 MTS shall implement and maintain reasonable and appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the Data.

4.3 HPN shall be responsible for ensuring that all Data provided to MTS is accurate, complete, and compliant with all applicable laws and regulations.

4.4 The Parties agree to execute a Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") in the form attached as Exhibit B, which shall govern the handling and protection of protected health information ("PHI") under this Contract.

ARTICLE 5: FEES AND EXPENSES

5.1 HPN shall pay to MTS the Fees and expenses set forth in the Statement of Work attached as Exhibit A, which shall be payable within thirty (30) days of receipt of an invoice from MTS.

5.2 The Fees shall be as follows:

(a) Consulting services: $250,000 per year, payable in quarterly installments of $62,500;

(b) Implementation services: $500,000, payable in two installments of $250,000, due upon completion of the implementation phase and upon completion of the testing phase;

(c) Support services: $100,000 per year, payable in monthly installments of $8,333;

(d) Customization services: $200,000, payable in two installments of $100,000, due upon completion of the design phase and upon completion of the development phase.

5.3 HPN shall reimburse MTS for all reasonable and necessary expenses incurred in connection with the provision of the Services, including but not limited to travel, lodging, and meal expenses.

5.4 All Fees and expenses shall be payable in United States dollars, without deduction or setoff.

ARTICLE 6: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 MTS retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Intellectual Property Rights in the MTS Software and all Deliverables.

6.2 HPN shall not, and shall not permit any third party to, use the MTS Software or any Deliverables for any purpose other than as specifically authorized in this Contract.

6.3 HPN shall not, and shall not permit any third party to, challenge or dispute MTS's ownership of the Intellectual Property Rights in the MTS Software or any Deliverables.

ARTICLE 7: CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 The Parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information disclosed by one Party to the other Party.

7.2 The receiving Party shall not, and shall not permit any third party to, disclose or use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than as specifically authorized in this Contract.

7.3 The receiving Party shall take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect the confidentiality of the Confidential Information, including but not limited to storing the Confidential Information in a secure location and restricting access to the Confidential Information to authorized personnel.

ARTICLE 8: REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

8.1 MTS represents and warrants that:

(a) it has the right and authority to enter into this Contract and to grant the licenses and rights granted herein;

(b) the MTS Software and all Deliverables shall be free from defects in material and workmanship for a period of one (1) year from the date of delivery;

(c) the MTS Software and all Deliverables shall conform to the specifications and requirements set forth in the Statement of Work attached as Exhibit A.

8.2 HPN represents and warrants that:

(a) it has the right and authority to enter into this Contract and to receive the Services and the licenses and rights granted herein;

(b) all Data provided to MTS shall be accurate, complete, and compliant with all applicable laws and regulations.

ARTICLE 9: INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 MTS shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless HPN, its officers, directors, employees, and agents, from and against any and all claims, demands, losses, damages, and expenses (including but not limited to reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any breach of this Contract by MTS;

(b) any negligence or willful misconduct by MTS or its officers, directors, employees, or agents;

(c) any claim that the MTS Software or any Deliverables infringe any Intellectual Property Rights of any third party.

9.2 HPN shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless MTS, its officers, directors, employees, and agents, from and against any and all claims, demands, losses, damages, and expenses (including but not limited to reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any breach of this Contract by HPN;

(b) any negligence or willful misconduct by HPN or its officers, directors, employees, or agents;

(c) any claim that any Data provided by HPN infringes any Intellectual Property Rights of any third party.

ARTICLE 10: TERMINATION

10.1 This Contract may be terminated by either Party upon written notice to the other Party, as follows:

(a) if either Party breaches any material term or condition of this Contract, and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days of receipt of written notice from the other Party;

(b) if either Party becomes insolvent, files a petition in bankruptcy, or makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors;

(c) if either Party is acquired by or merges with any third party, and such acquisition or merger results in a change of control of such Party.

10.2 Upon termination of this Contract, HPN shall immediately cease using the MTS Software and all Deliverables, and shall return to MTS all Confidential Information and all other materials and property belonging to MTS.

10.3 The following provisions shall survive termination of this Contract: Articles 6, 7, 8, 9, and 11.

ARTICLE 11: LIABILITY

11.1 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, SPECIAL, INCIDENTAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO DAMAGES FOR LOSS OF PROFITS, BUSINESS INTERRUPTION, OR LOSS OF DATA, ARISING OUT OF OR RESULTING FROM THIS CONTRACT, EVEN IF SUCH PARTY HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

11.2 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY'S LIABILITY UNDER THIS CONTRACT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY HPN TO MTS UNDER THIS CONTRACT DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO SUCH LIABILITY.

11.3 THE LIMITATIONS OF LIABILITY SET FORTH IN THIS ARTICLE 11 SHALL NOT APPLY TO DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF OR RESULTING FROM:

(a) GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT BY EITHER PARTY;

(b) BREACH OF ARTICLE 7 (CONFIDENTIALITY);

(c) INFRINGEMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS.

ARTICLE 12: FORCE MAJEURE

12.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Contract, if such failure or delay is due to any cause beyond the reasonable control of such Party, including but not limited to:

(a) natural disasters, such as earthquakes, hurricanes, or floods;

(b) war, riot, or civil unrest;

(c) strikes or labor disputes;

(d) epidemics or pandemics;

(e) failure of public utilities or common carriers.

12.2 In the event of a force majeure, the Parties shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects of such event and to resume performance of their obligations as soon as reasonably possible.

ARTICLE 13: GOVERNING LAW

13.1 This Contract shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any principles of conflicts of law.

13.2 Any dispute or claim arising out of or resulting from this Contract shall be resolved through binding arbitration in accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration Association, as amended from time to time.

ARTICLE 14: ENTIRE AGREEMENT

14.1 This Contract constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements or understandings, whether written or oral.

14.2 This Contract may not be amended or modified except in writing signed by both Parties.

EXHIBIT A: STATEMENT OF WORK

The following services shall be provided by MTS to HPN under this Contract:

1. Consulting services: MTS shall provide consulting services to HPN for a period of twelve (12) months, commencing on the Effective Date, to assist HPN in the implementation and utilization of the MTS Software.

2. Implementation services: MTS shall provide implementation services to HPN for a period of six (6) months, commencing on the Effective Date, to assist HPN in the installation, configuration, and customization of the MTS Software.

3. Support services: MTS shall provide support services to HPN for a period of twelve (12) months, commencing on the Effective Date, to assist HPN in the use and maintenance of the MTS Software.

4. Customization services: MTS shall provide customization services to HPN for a period of three (3) months, commencing on the Effective Date, to assist HPN in the customization of the MTS Software to meet its specific needs and requirements.

EXHIBIT B: BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

This Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") is made and entered into on this 15th day of March, 2023, by and between Medical Technology Solutions, Inc. ("MTS") and Healthcare Provider Network, LLC ("HPN").

WHEREAS, MTS is a business associate of HPN, as defined in the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA");

WHEREAS, MTS shall have access to protected health information ("PHI") in connection with the provision of services to HPN;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

1. MTS shall comply with all applicable requirements of HIPAA, including but not limited to the use and disclosure of PHI.

2. MTS shall implement and maintain reasonable and appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of PHI.

3. MTS shall report to HPN any security incident or breach of unsecured PHI, as required by HIPAA.

4. MTS shall ensure that all Subcontractors and agents of MTS comply with the terms and conditions of this BAA.

5. This BAA shall terminate upon termination of the Contract between MTS and HPN.
