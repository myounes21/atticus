Q. Mr. Wilson, can you describe your role in the events leading up to the incident that is the subject of this lawsuit?

A. Yes. On January 10, 2022, I was working on a financial analysis project for one of our clients, when I received an email from my supervisor, Mr. Michael Johnson, requesting that I meet with him in his office. During the meeting, Mr. Johnson informed me that the company was considering a potential merger with another company, and he asked me to prepare a financial analysis of the proposed transaction.

Q. Did you have any concerns or reservations about the proposed merger?

A. Yes, I had some concerns about the potential risks and synergies of the merger. I expressed these concerns to Mr. Johnson during our meeting, and he asked me to include them in my analysis.

Q. Can you describe the analysis you prepared?

A. I prepared a comprehensive financial analysis of the proposed merger, including a review of the financial statements of both companies, an analysis of the potential synergies and cost savings, and a discussion of the potential risks and challenges associated with the transaction. I also prepared a financial model that projected the potential financial performance of the combined company over a 5-year period.

Q. Did you discuss your analysis with anyone else at the company?

A. Yes, I discussed my analysis with several colleagues, including Mr. Johnson, Ms. Emily Chen, and Mr. David Lee. I also presented my findings to the company's executive committee on January 20, 2022.

Q. What was the reaction of the executive committee to your analysis?

A. The executive committee had a lively discussion about the proposed merger, and there were differing opinions about the potential benefits and risks of the transaction. Some members of the committee expressed concerns about the potential risks, while others were more optimistic about the potential benefits.

MR. JOHN D. LEE, ESQ.: Objection, your honor. This line of questioning is hearsay.

MR. MARK S. THOMPSON, ESQ.: I disagree, your honor. The witness is testifying about his own conversations and interactions with the executive committee.

THE COURT: Sustained. The witness may not testify about the statements or opinions of others.

Q. Mr. Wilson, did you have any further involvement in the events leading up to the incident?

A. Yes, I was asked to prepare an updated financial analysis of the proposed merger, which I completed on February 1, 2022. I also attended a meeting with the company's board of directors on February 10, 2022, where the proposed merger was discussed.

Q. Can you describe what happened at the meeting with the board of directors?

A. The meeting was attended by the company's board of directors, as well as several members of the executive committee. The proposed merger was discussed, and I presented my updated financial analysis. The board of directors voted to approve the merger, subject to certain conditions.

Q. Did you have any concerns or reservations about the board's decision?

A. Yes, I had some concerns about the potential risks of the merger, which I expressed to the board of directors during the meeting.

Q. Can you describe the events that occurred after the meeting with the board of directors?

A. After the meeting, I was asked to assist with the integration of the two companies, which involved coordinating with various teams and departments to ensure a smooth transition. I worked closely with the company's IT department to ensure that the financial systems of the two companies were integrated properly.

Q. Did you notice anything unusual or suspicious during the integration process?

A. Yes, I noticed that some of the financial data from the acquired company was missing or incomplete, which made it difficult to prepare accurate financial reports. I brought this to the attention of my supervisor, Mr. Johnson, who assured me that it was being investigated.

Q. Did you have any further involvement in the events leading up to the incident?

A. Yes, I was asked to prepare a financial report for the company's quarterly earnings release, which was scheduled for March 15, 2022. I worked closely with the company's accounting department to ensure that the financial statements were accurate and complete.

Q. Can you describe what happened on the day of the quarterly earnings release?

A. On the morning of March 15, 2022, I arrived at the office early to finalize the financial report. As I was reviewing the report, I noticed that some of the financial data was incorrect, which would have a significant impact on the company's earnings per share. I immediately brought this to the attention of my supervisor, Mr. Johnson, who instructed me to correct the error and reissue the report.

Q. Did you correct the error and reissue the report?

A. Yes, I corrected the error and reissued the report. However, the corrected report was not released to the public until several hours after the original report, which had already been released.

MR. JOHN D. LEE, ESQ.: Objection, your honor. This line of questioning is irrelevant to the case at hand.

MR. MARK S. THOMPSON, ESQ.: I disagree, your honor. The witness's testimony about the events leading up to the incident is relevant to the case.

THE COURT: Overruled. The witness may continue to answer the questions.

Q. Mr. Wilson, can you describe the reaction of the company's management to the incident?

A. The company's management was very concerned about the incident and the potential impact on the company's reputation and financial performance. An investigation was launched to determine the cause of the error and to identify measures to prevent similar errors in the future.

Q. Did you participate in the investigation?

A. Yes, I was interviewed by the investigators and provided information about my role in the events leading up to the incident.

Q. Can you describe the findings of the investigation?

A. The investigation found that the error was caused by a combination of human error and system failures. The company implemented several measures to prevent similar errors in the future, including additional training for employees and enhancements to the financial reporting system.

Q. Mr. Wilson, do you have any personal knowledge about the incident that is the subject of this lawsuit?

A. Yes, I have personal knowledge about the events leading up to the incident, as well as the incident itself.

Q. Can you describe your personal knowledge about the incident?

A. As I mentioned earlier, I was involved in the preparation of the financial analysis of the proposed merger, and I was also involved in the integration of the two companies. I was also responsible for preparing the financial report for the company's quarterly earnings release.

Q. Did you have any direct involvement in the incident?

A. Yes, I had direct involvement in the incident, as I was the person who discovered the error in the financial report and brought it to the attention of my supervisor.
