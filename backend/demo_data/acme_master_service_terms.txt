ACME LOGISTICS - MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT SUMMARY

Counterparties:
- Client: Acme Logistics, Inc.
- Counsel: Northstar Counsel LLP

Execution and governing law:
- Effective date: 2024-01-08
- Governing law: New York

Commercial terms:
- Payment terms: Net 30 from invoice issue date.
- Invoice dispute window: 10 business days from invoice receipt.
- Late fees: 1.0% per month on undisputed overdue balances.

Scope and change control:
- Any scope change requires written notice and a signed change order.
- Email notice is acceptable only if acknowledged by both parties.

Confidentiality and data handling:
- Confidentiality obligations survive termination for 3 years.
- Client data must be stored in systems restricted to assigned legal team members.

Termination and transition:
- Either party may terminate for material breach after a 15-day cure period.
- On termination, counsel must provide transfer-ready work product within 7 business days.

Dispute resolution:
- Escalation: project leads -> general counsel -> executive review.
- If unresolved within 30 days, parties may initiate litigation in New York courts.
