DEPOSITION OF MAYELLA EWELL

Case: Finch v. State
Date: November 24

Q: Where were you on the evening of November 21st?
A: I was at home.

Q: Did you invite Tom Robinson inside?
A: No, he came in.

Q: What happened?
A: He attacked me.

Q: Which hand did he use?
A: I do not remember.

Q: Did you scream?
A: Yes.

Q: Who came after?
A: My father.

Q: Had he been to your house before?
A: Yes, to help sometimes.
