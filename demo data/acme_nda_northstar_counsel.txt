
**NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2024, by and between:

**Acme Logistics Inc.**, a corporation organized under the laws of the State of New York, with its principal place of business at 450 Park Avenue, New York, NY 10022 ("Disclosing Party"),

and

**Northstar Counsel LLP**, a limited liability partnership organized under the laws of the State of New York, with its principal place of business at 200 West 57th Street, New York, NY 10019 ("Receiving Party").

---

**ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS**

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether orally, in writing, or by any other means, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, without limitation, trade secrets, business strategies, financial data, client lists, proprietary software, and operational procedures.

1.2 "Permitted Purpose" means the evaluation and rendering of legal services by the Receiving Party in connection with the Acme Logistics data breach investigation initiated in February 2024.

1.3 "Representative" means any employee, officer, director, partner, attorney, accountant, or advisor of a party who has a legitimate need to access the Confidential Information for the Permitted Purpose.

---

**ARTICLE 2 — OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY**

2.1 The Receiving Party agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and to take all reasonable precautions to protect such information, using no less than the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

2.2 The Receiving Party shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, except to Representatives who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less restrictive than those set forth in this Agreement.

2.3 The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Permitted Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever.

2.4 The Receiving Party shall promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing upon becoming aware of any unauthorized disclosure, misappropriation, or breach of this Agreement.

2.5 The Receiving Party shall maintain and document administrative, technical, and physical safeguards reasonably designed to protect Confidential Information from unauthorized access, acquisition, use, or disclosure.

2.6 If Confidential Information includes incident-response records, forensic artifacts, or security architecture materials, the Receiving Party shall restrict access to personnel directly assigned to the Permitted Purpose and shall maintain an internal access log for a minimum of twenty-four (24) months.

---

**ARTICLE 3 — EXCLUSIONS**

3.1 The obligations set forth in Article 2 shall not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;

(b) was rightfully known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure without restriction;

(c) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction on disclosure;

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Confidential Information; or

(e) is required to be disclosed by applicable law, regulation, or court order, provided that the Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice and cooperates with any efforts to seek a protective order.

---

**ARTICLE 4 — TERM AND TERMINATION**

4.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and shall remain in effect for a period of three (3) years, unless earlier terminated by either party upon thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party.

4.2 Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, or upon written request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all materials containing Confidential Information, and shall certify such destruction in writing if requested.

4.3 Notwithstanding the foregoing, the obligations of confidentiality with respect to any trade secrets shall survive termination of this Agreement for so long as such information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

4.4 Following termination, each party shall cooperate in good faith to confirm whether any litigation hold, regulatory inquiry, or insurer requirement permits retention of otherwise returnable materials. Any retained materials shall remain subject to this Agreement until lawfully released.

---

**ARTICLE 5 — REMEDIES**

5.1 The Receiving Party acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to the Disclosing Party for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek equitable relief, including injunction and specific performance, in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity.

5.2 In the event of any legal action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

---

**ARTICLE 6 — GENERAL PROVISIONS**

6.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

6.2 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements, whether written or oral.

6.3 **Amendment.** This Agreement may not be amended or modified except by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

6.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

6.5 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.

6.6 **Notice.** Any notice required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by (i) recognized overnight courier, (ii) certified mail, return receipt requested, or (iii) electronic mail with confirmation of receipt to the notice contacts listed in Schedule A.

6.7 **No Waiver.** No failure or delay by either party in exercising any right under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver of that right.

---

**ARTICLE 7 - INCIDENT COMMUNICATION PROTOCOL**

7.1 If either party identifies a suspected unauthorized disclosure involving Confidential Information, the noticing party shall provide initial written notice without unreasonable delay and, where feasible, within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.

7.2 The noticing party's initial notice shall include, to the extent known at the time: (a) a summary of the incident, (b) categories of information potentially affected, (c) date and time range of suspected access, and (d) immediate containment actions.

7.3 The parties shall designate one legal lead and one technical lead each for coordinated incident communications during any active investigation.

7.4 Nothing in this Article requires disclosure of privileged attorney work product beyond what is required by applicable law or court order.
