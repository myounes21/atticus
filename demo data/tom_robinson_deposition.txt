DEPOSITION OF TOM ROBINSON

Case: Finch v. State
Date: November 25

Q: Why were you near the Ewell residence?
A: I was passing by.

Q: Did you enter the house?
A: Yes, she asked me to help.

Q: What happened inside?
A: She spoke to me, then her father appeared.

Q: Did you assault her?
A: No, sir.

Q: Can you use your left arm?
A: No, it is useless.

Q: Have you ever harmed her?
A: Never.
